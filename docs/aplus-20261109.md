# Amazon A+ pages and the shared technology visual rule (9 November 2026)

Background: the 8 November audit (`work/aplus-audit-20261108/report.md`) found that the
"Amazon A+ Content" card made three plain 1080 × 1080 squares with no words, planned module by
module, with no Amazon text fields, no alt text and no file-size rule. The user's decisions of
9 November are built here. No paid generation was run to build or test this.

## What the user does

1. Choose **Amazon A+ Content** (home picker, brief box or Website & marketplaces → Marketplace A+ Images).
2. The brief opens on **Premium (recommended)**. The "Image preset" select also offers **Basic** and
   **Brand Story** (and, further down, the earlier single modules for replacing one image).
3. The brief shows the module list with exact sizes, the image count and what is delivered.
4. Pick the product, write the brief, press review: the INR estimate for every image is shown
   before anything is paid for. Press create: ONE job makes the whole page.
5. Download the image set: besides the images, an `amazon-a-plus/` folder holds Amazon-ready files and
   the copy deck.

## Module sets

| Type | Modules (in story order) | Images |
|---|---|---|
| Premium (default) | Hero 1464×600 + mobile 600×450 · Technology 1464×600 + 600×450 · Benefits carousel 3 × (1464×600 + 600×450) · Lifestyle 1464×600 + 600×450 · Two detail images 2 × 650×350 · FAQ (text only) | 14 |
| Basic | Header 970×600 · Overlay 970×300 (technology) · Three images 3 × 300×300 · Highlights 300×300 | 6 |
| Brand Story | Background 1464×625 + mobile 463×625 · 3 story cards 362×453 | 5 |

Left out for now (named in the brief and the result): own-brand comparison (Premium) and comparison
chart (Basic) need other products of the same brand; the Brand Story About card (logo upload) and
four-product card need the saved logo and other products.

## How it works (new jobs only)

- `public/creative-ai/studio-model.js`: `aplusPageTypes`, `aplusSlots`, `aplusSummary`; the three page
  presets live in `amazonTemplates` ("Amazon A+ page — …"). `generationProfile` gives every image a
  custom profile `custom:recipe-marketplace-module-amazon-aplus-<type>-NN`, per-page `jobInputs`
  (`formatIntent` with one shared `familyId` → one planning group, and `aplus` module info) and a logo
  map (logo only on the hero).
- `studio-generation.js` pins `aplusVersion: 1`, the page inputs, the logo map and the user's own
  wording (`aplusSuppliedText`: the brief, plus offer and terms only when the asset says it has an offer).
  `lean-policy.configure` copies the pin into the job snapshot (`provider-config.snapshot`).
- `lib/creative-ai/runtime/aplus.js` adds to the planning packet: the module list, per-page text rules,
  Amazon text units with character limits, FAQ (Premium) and the technology evidence. One planning rule
  (`aplus-page-v1`) shapes the plan and stays out of image prompts; each page gets a short execution
  line (its `structure`).
- **On-image text**: wide panels (970 px or wider) carry the product name, a headline of 8 words or fewer
  and up to three chips (5 blocks, 24 words at most); mobile versions reuse 1–3 of the desktop page's
  exact text blocks in larger type; small images carry no text or a 1–3 word label; the Brand Story
  background carries no words (Amazon places its own logo, headline and cards on it — a deliberate
  exception to the "wide panel" rule). One logo, on the hero.
- **Wording**: links, QR codes, contact details, customer reviews/ratings, best-seller/top-rated and
  competitor names are removed from the plan (recorded in `lean-aplus-<key>.json`; the saved planner
  output itself is never rewritten). Offers, prices, EMI, discounts and warranty/guarantee/trial wording
  stay only when the brief supplies them word for word; time-sensitive words also need a supplied end
  date. When offer or warranty wording stays, the result and package say "Amazon may reject prices or
  offers in A+ content." (or the warranty equivalent).
- **Amazon text fields and alt text**: the plan carries `aplus.native` (headline/body per module unit)
  and `altText` per image (100 characters or fewer) and, for Premium, 3–5 FAQ pairs citing facts.
  Limits: Standard header headline 150 and overlay 70/300 and alt text 100 are Amazon's documented
  limits; all other caps (70/300 for Premium modules, 120/300 for FAQ, 50/150 for Brand Story cards,
  600 for the header body) are conservative Studio caps, shorter than Amazon may allow.
- **Files**: `aplus.amazonFile` keeps an RGB PNG when it is 2 MB or less, otherwise makes a JPEG at the
  same pixels (quality stepped down until it fits). The image-set download (`delivery-packages.js`,
  static set) and the campaign pack zip add `amazon-a-plus/upload/*`, `copy-deck.json`,
  `copy-deck.csv` and a README. Per-page size reaches image generation (`generationSize`) and
  `exact-canvas.fit` as for every lean page.
- **Estimate**: `studio-estimate.aplusEstimate` scales completed one-design lean runs of the same brand
  per image (planning cost counted once at the low end and twice at the high end, because one plan
  covers the page) times the page's image count. On 9 Nov saved runs gave about ₹82–₹192 (Duroflex)
  and ₹100–₹261 (Sleepyhead) for a Premium page; this is not a quote.
- Results show the notes (`job.aplus.notes`, shown through `M.resultNotes`).
- The brief reader maps "A+", "A plus" and "Amazon A+" to the A+ card (Premium page by default).

## Technology visual v1 (shared engine rule, every format)

User scope addition (9 Nov 2026): "Technology visual should always have the technology mentioned for the
SKU, preferably taking the image of the tech from the SKU's images."

- `lib/creative-ai/runtime/technology-visual.js`, pinned for every new lean job
  (`technologyVisualVersion: 1`). It applies when the brief's message type is **Technology**, or a page is
  a technology or feature-explainer page (the A+ technology module uses the same helpers).
- **Name**: the plan must name the technology word for word from a cited exact-SKU technology fact; a
  generic benefit is rejected. Offers, warranty, trial and replacement facts never count.
- **Picture**: `studio-generation` pins the SKU's official technology picture
  (`technologyImage`: A+ content first, then feature tiles, then supporting media from the same brand's
  saved product snapshot, never icons or gallery photos). Planning re-reads it from that brand and
  product and checks its hash; another brand's or another product's picture, or a changed file, is
  rejected and recorded (`pictureError`). It is attached to the planner and to the technology page(s)
  only, with the instruction to show only what it shows.
- **Fallbacks** (noted on the result): no technology fact → the plan uses the strongest supported benefit;
  a fact but no picture → fact text only, no layers or cutaways.
- **Defaults**: with message type Technology, the brief form (`M.applyTechnologyDefault`) and the brief
  reader (`finish`) suggest the product's technology features; they stay changeable.

## Historical jobs

Jobs, packets, plans, request identities, reviews and budgets saved without `aplusVersion` /
`technologyVisualVersion` are unchanged. The earlier single A+ module presets keep their image-only,
module-by-module contract.

## Limits

- Not verified with a paid run: model compliance with the text rules, readability at phone size, the
  real cost and whether 14 images finish inside the 45-minute window.
- The technology picture is chosen automatically (A+ media naming the technology first); there is no
  picker for it yet. Page-name detection of "feature-explainer" pages is a word match.
- Amazon acceptance, Seller/Vendor Central upload and Premium eligibility are not checked.
