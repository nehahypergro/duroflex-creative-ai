### Repair-mask and reviewer-evidence follow-up

New image-only product repairs expand observed product rectangles by eight pixels to admit a controlled transition. Expansion is rejected if it hits protected people, overlays, the image edge or the existing 35% area cap. These are still observed rectangles, not semantic segmentation. Eight-pixel interior blending preserves the original outer boundary gradient and every pixel outside the admitted mask; the edited interior retains its texture without a whole-product harmonic colour adjustment. Text/logo removal retains the existing restoration method. Pixel proofs now record transition width and boundary-gradient difference, which are diagnostics, not visual approval. Interior seams, ghosting and malformed product parts still require final review.

Instructions distinguish actual edge clipping from proximity or foreground props. Missing/out-of-bounds evidence becomes a verification problem; it never silently passes the image.
