Remaining issues and uncertainties
High priority: readiness for large campaigns is too slow (~3 minutes in the isolated 30-asset test). Serial repeated preparation/library reads are candidates for profiling and request-scoped reuse; broad concurrency changes were not made without isolating bottlenecks and invalidation rules.
Release verification remains incomplete: admin brief upload/autofill, progress/operations and some editor/browser tests time out or fail. Some assertions reference removed controls; others require reproduction before changing product behavior. Full suites have not been rerun green after the targeted fixes.
Historical replay fixtures are missing (ENOENT) for several legacy tests. User-authorized historical cleanup may explain their absence; no fixtures were fabricated and no saved work was deleted.
Legacy tests still expect one option, older model defaults, or older readiness labels. Some mocks lack current store interfaces. Those failures are not evidence that the new two-option workflow should be reverted.
Art provenance and legacy editable composition checks still have mismatches needing targeted investigation. Do not loosen correctness checks just to pass old tests.
Resume snapshots cover completed handoffs, not arbitrary future code compatibility. Old records and incomplete plans require compatibility fallbacks; future schema changes should retain versioned validation/migrations.
Potential risk, not demonstrated bug: DNS validation and fetch resolution are separate; investigate rebinding exposure before claiming the network boundary is fully hardened.
Potential risk, not demonstrated bug: malformed editor-share records could interrupt share listing; needs a focused fixture before changing behavior.
Potential risk: large archive/product snapshots and retention can consume significant resources. No concurrent-upload stress test or destructive pruning was performed.
Current aggregate campaign-budget accounting still depends on existing operator/preflight controls; this review does not certify a new global budget enforcement service.
Requested output dimensions/provider constraints remain a separate known issue and were not changed in this patch.
