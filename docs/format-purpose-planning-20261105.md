# Format-purpose planning and the raw experiment

## Confirmed cause

The earlier raw collection adapter reused a source generation package, generated
copy and visual direction. It dispatched images without running fresh strategy,
concept, copy or art planning. This encouraged banner-style headlines and room
styling across unrelated formats. That adapter remains only for historical
version 1/2 batches and their saved evidence.

Main Studio already ran those planning stages. Its shared instructions did not
explicitly distinguish independent asset purposes from continuity inside a set.
The saved main-flow replays available for this change are single-placement jobs;
they do not prove that every main-flow campaign had the observed repetition.

## New behavior

- New jobs pin `formatIntentVersion: 1`. Strategy receives each format's
  communication purpose, viewing context and relationship to other placements.
  Copy and art planning retain this guidance. No arbitrary palette or headline
  variation is forced; explicit continuity, exact copy and brand rules win.
- New raw collection batches use version 3 and one normal Studio planning job
  per asset family, with `rawArtworkExperimentVersion: 2`. Independent formats
  plan separately. Pages and ratio variants of a set stay in one job and receive
  an ordered purpose/message/composition plan.
- The experiment uses the validated generation package after shared copy/layout
  reconciliation. It then saves the model's original raster bytes, without
  reviewer calls, editable reconstruction, background repair or resizing.
- Source reuse is limited to the authoritative brief, scoped brand guidance and
  exact product evidence. The requested format explicitly replaces the earlier
  format/dimensions and execution policy. Generated source copy, room palette,
  layouts and old composition checkpoints are not new requirements.
- Five rolling family slots remain; a failed family does not stop other families.
  Existing cost admission, request identity and provider reconciliation apply.
  A stopped worker does not silently resubmit an ambiguous paid request.

## Compatibility

Existing jobs, edits, verdicts, costs and generated images were not rewritten.
Unpinned historical jobs retain their planning contracts. Main Studio's editable
and reviewed delivery path is unchanged; reviewer bypass is limited to the
explicit raw experiment version. Native carousel/PDP planners remain in place.

Raster previews are not certified print files, videos or native documents. The
experiment continues to disclose those format limitations. Brand inspiration
remains verbal; exact SKU and identity references remain visual when required.

## Verification

112 focused tests passed, including both-brand catalogue admission, image-only
copy contracts, original-byte preservation, safe checkpoint reuse, actual
activity-ledger integration, independent failure handling and rolling refill.
Existing print tests needed local mock-server permission and a test-only DNS
stub for example.com; no provider calls were made by those tests.

Saved Duroflex and Sleepyhead strategy-input replays passed without changing
their historical files. Evidence: `work/format-purpose-20261105/`.

Integration checks caught and corrected: oversized individual strategy constraint
strings, missing collection activity scope, and a preview filename incompatible
with the public image allowlist. They are covered by regression checks.

The supervised local server was restarted after confirming no active jobs.
A new Duroflex Octave retail flyer is the representative live test:
`raw-web-e13e4d90e285df2eb0521d5a`, job
`creative-30065385-7860-4eda-8d09-57ba36018d55`.
This first attempt stopped during reconciliation, before any image call. It
incorrectly inherited editable font-file requirements and print-production PPI
as requirements for a raw raster preview. Its strategy also retained the earlier
website destination as a structured desired action. The attempt and paid
planning receipts remain preserved.

The follow-up fixes make the current asset direction the primary brief, retain
the earlier brief/action/destination as attributed background, and explicitly
state the raster-preview workflow. Print layout retains trim/panel/safety
geometry while production/proof PPI stays in the saved slot metadata, outside
the preview's generation requirements. Blocked dispatch now surfaces the actual
dependency reasons. Main editable and print-production checks are unchanged.

The second attempt, `raw-web-adbdedcfc0156216ad0183eb`, job
`creative-c7188093-bb2b-4b6c-8958-44977558e8b3`, stopped at strategy. The compact
packet omitted the execution-asset inventory along with pixels, allowing a
repair response to incorrectly infer missing product/logo assets. Its initial
response also omitted the contract-required research request for an unverified
destination. Strategy now receives a small scoped metadata inventory (no pixels),
and instructions explain the existing destination-uncertainty contract, including
nonessential requests when a general action needs no specific store address.
The validator was not relaxed. Both-brand saved inventory replays pass.

The third attempt completed: `raw-web-2cd60300be2b737f78212f89`, job
`creative-10e2039e-fa2c-4c92-aea6-1993ae1d2baa`. Five reasoning calls and one image
call completed without retries, in 288.238 seconds. Strategy chose in-store
exploration; the image shows “Meet Octave Honey,” a separate supported benefit
panel and “Explore Octave in store.” The browser gallery and preview displayed
the saved 848 × 1200 original. No browser download check was performed.

The result still uses a room scene and sage wall. This verifies the corrected
planning/dispatch path, not visual diversity or quality acceptance across all
formats. Raw output remains explicitly unreviewed, noneditable and not approved
for print production. Main-flow editable/review paths remain enabled.

Timing, usage and estimated INR costs for all three attempts are recorded in
`work/format-purpose-20261105/live-verification.json`; actual billing remains
unknown. Original failed attempts and their receipts were preserved. Full visual
validation of other independent formats and coordinated sets remains outstanding.
