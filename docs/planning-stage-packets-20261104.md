# Strategy and concept planning packets — 4 November 2026

The first rollout covers strategy and concept planning. New jobs pin `planningPacketVersion: 1`; existing jobs and idempotent resubmissions retain their original contract. Model routing, reasoning effort, output allowances, full saved inputs, validators, budgets and paid recovery limits are unchanged.

## Strategy

Receives the assignment (including audience, objective, supplied customer insight, CTA, formats and exact requirements), full fact wording and qualifiers, mandatory guardian requirements, preferences, relevant non-visual findings, conflicts, and the referenced source records with their authority/coverage. Repeated audit facts resolve to those records instead of duplicating them. Identical goal/brief text is represented once. It receives no raster attachments.

The new instruction section asks for the proposition, message, benefits and approach. It omits full generic examples and production/reconstruction/export instructions from the previous base prompt. The existing decision contract, dynamic correction feedback, brand boundary and planning blocking policy remain applied. Insights without evidence remain hypotheses.

## Concept planning

Receives the selected strategy brief and its assumptions/dependencies directly, rather than the complete earlier strategy input and alternative analyses. Includes the assignment, full fact/rule wording, visual/verbal guidance, conflicts, applicable format constraints, approved resource inventory and source authority.

Exact selected SKU photographs, approved logo references and explicitly selected/mandatory references retain their pixels. Other inspiration needs explicit stage applicability, with at most two optional references fitting the remaining attachment capacity. Unselected bedroom/library examples are not attached by default. Required references are never silently evicted to meet the cap. Missing selected pixels, conflicting identities and foreign-brand assets fail before dispatch.

The response schema restricts source and visual-reference citations to this packet, preserves the distinction between creative references and product/logo execution assets, and is checked locally before the existing full decoder/semantic validator. IDs do not substitute for fact/rule wording or attached pixels.

## Deliberate conservative limits

Unknown applicability of factual evidence or requirements is retained. This rollout does not use keyword guesses to discard an unresolved conflict or infer that a user requirement is irrelevant. Facts still lacking reliable SKU/format scope can therefore remain in the packet. This is not a blanket removal of all brand background. Required dimensions and layout constraints remain verbatim when their origin cannot safely be separated from user requirements.

Other stages (verification, reusable brand preparation, collection, copy, art, reconciliation, generation, reconstruction, review, recovery and export) retain their existing behavior; their next packet migrations are separate changes. The inspiration change here applies to concepts, not a claim that all later image-selection paths have been migrated.
