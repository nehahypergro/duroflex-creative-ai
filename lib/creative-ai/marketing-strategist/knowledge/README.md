# Curated foundations, version 1

This pack turns principles into decision guidance, rather than a fictional senior-marketer persona. `pack.json` is loaded into every assembled strategist context. Each principle includes source IDs, a short paraphrase, editorial application guidance, limitations, a synthetic worked example and a counterexample. All examples are fictional and have no reported outcomes. The pack has not been validated by qualified marketing reviewers.

The sources were checked on 4 October 2026. Only short original summaries are saved; no books, papers or screenshots are reproduced. Primary articles below are original research/theory hosted by their authors' foundation or university mirrors. Framework-owner and institute pages are primary practitioner sources, not substitutes for underlying study evidence.

- [Strategyzer: Value proposition](https://www.strategyzer.com/value-proposition): customer needs, value exchange and feature/benefit distinctions.
- [Strategyzer: Testing value propositions](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/ways-to-test-your-value-proposition-and-business-model): evidence types, observed behavior and experiment limitations. Objective/journey guidance is an editorial adaptation for assignment strategy.
- [Ehrenberg-Bass: Category entry points](https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/identifying-and-prioritising-category-entry-points): buying situations and brand memory. No category-specific survey results are included.
- [Ehrenberg-Bass: Distinctive asset measurement](https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/distinctive-asset): recognition, linkage and continuity. The institute's service description is not measurement of the user's assets.
- [Behavioural Insights Team: EAST](https://www.bi.team/publications/east-four-simple-ways-to-apply-behavioural-insights/): attention, ease and contextual intervention prompts. Static-creative applications are hypotheses, not predictions.
- [Ryan and Deci, 2000](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf): motivation and social conditions. The theory does not establish a particular audience's purchase motive.
- [Tversky and Kahneman, 1981](https://psychology.hanover.edu/classes/Cognition/papers/tversky81.pdf): framing and decisions under risk. The paper itself describes the model as approximate and non-universal. Reassurance/trust guidance is an editorial application, not a claimed conversion result.
- [Markus and Kitayama, 1991](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Motivation/Markus_Kitayama_1991_Culture_and_the_self.pdf): cultural/social context and variation; avoid treating nationality as an individual psychological profile.

The suggested Brands of Distinction page could not be fetched; the accessible institute measurement page supplies the active brand-assets reference. The Nobel lecture page also returned 403; the accessible original framing paper supplies the active decision-framing reference. Failed references are recorded in `pack.json` and are not passed off as reviewed content.

Customer relevance, consistent recognition, established positioning, supported claims and communication hierarchy are governed jointly by these principles and the assignment/guardian contract. A principle cannot override a verified product limitation or explicit brand requirement. Missing customer evidence remains unknown. Knowledge about marketing does not become evidence about the user's brand.
