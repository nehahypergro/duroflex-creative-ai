# Creative director knowledge, version 1

The pack is loaded into assembled director context. These are contextual principles and editorial adaptations, not universal idea templates or proof of creative effectiveness. All examples are synthetic and annotated with counterexamples. No qualified reviewer evaluation has occurred.

Primary references checked on 4 October 2026:

- [Runco and Jaeger (2012)](https://disf.org/files/doc/2012runcojaegerstandarddefinition.pdf): original Creativity Research Journal article, hosted by DISF. It supplies the originality/appropriateness distinction; this is not campaign-outcome evidence.
- [Gentner (1983)](https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/Gentner83.2b.pdf): original Cognitive Science article at the author laboratory, supporting relational analogy. Application to a visual ad remains an editorial hypothesis.
- [Green and Brock (2000)](https://researchconnect.buffalo.edu/en/publications/the-role-of-transportation-in-the-persuasiveness-of-public-narrat/): original article's author-university record and abstract. Only the abstract was reviewed; narrative-reading findings cannot be assumed to apply to a single static.
- [Simons Visual Cognition Lab](https://simonslab.com/research.html): researcher summary of selective attention. It does not supply static-ad comprehension, recall or conversion rates.
- [Ehrenberg-Bass Distinctive Asset Measurement](https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/distinctive-asset): primary institute service summary; asset recognition and linkage require actual measurement.
- [Stanford d.school Project Scoping Guide](https://dschool.stanford.edu/tools/design-project-scoping-guide): framework-owner practice guidance for context and iteration, not independent validation of these concepts.

Each entry separates `sourceSummary` from `application`, `limits`, `syntheticExample`, and `counterexample`. Strategic constraints originate in the validated strategist/guardian packet, not in these academic/practice references. Review source scope before using a principle to justify a concept. Future qualified review should separately assess factual/brand failures, clarity, relevance, distinction and feasibility, preserving disagreements; future outcomes require appropriate evaluation rather than assertions that a concept will win.
