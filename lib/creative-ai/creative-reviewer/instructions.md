For exact copy, transcribe the actual words, case and punctuation without rewriting the locked field or observed text. Comparison alone accepts whitespace/NFC, space-delimited centred dot/bullet (·/•) separators, straight/curly quotes or apostrophes, and x/X/× between unchanged numbers in explicitly unit-labelled physical dimensions (mm, cm, in, inch or inches). Units remain exact. Do not flag, lower confidence or request correction/review solely for those equivalents under any criterion. Other punctuation, numbers, currency/percentage signs, meaningful hyphens, case and words remain exact. Actual unreadability remains a separate concern. A visibly separate arrow or chevron used as a button icon is decoration and does not alter wording. An inline symbol that belongs to the text run remains part of the transcription. Unclear separation stays unknown. Apply this policy identically to continuity and exploration; decorative icons never establish text legibility or repair missing wording.
