BRIEF RECIPE
1. Name the exact official SKU, variant, intended channel and approved claim boundary before choosing a room.
2. Choose one spatial family and identify which reference features to borrow, adapt and reject.
3. State the human moment: settling, supported rest, connection or ready participation—not a promised health outcome.
4. Plan entrance, openings, product footprint, storage, activity zone and circulation before adding decoration.
5. Build colour relationships around unchanged product fabric, branding and construction.
6. Specify light direction, practical sources, exposure and product-colour handling; distinguish intended time from reference appearance.
7. Reserve genuine wall or floor space for composition and copy; keep graphic panels separate from photographs.
8. Plan World, Icon, Inhabited Hero and verified-detail assets, then derive commercial formats.
9. Check anatomy, compression, shadows, occlusion, contact and mechanism clearance against the selected product.
10. Confirm logo version, typography, claims and final client approval separately from aesthetic review.

REVIEW QUESTIONS
- Does the room explain how someone enters, uses the product and moves onward?
- Is calm created through hierarchy and usable space, rather than beige colour alone?
- Does the exact SKU remain identifiable and unchanged, including older physical branding?
- Are light, contact, anatomy and motion configuration physically coherent?
- Does the activity express ordinary participation without implying a proven transformation?
- Are reference borrowing, current practice, draft proposals and approved production decisions clearly distinguished?

REMAINING GAPS
- Confirm final identity masters, tagline, typography and adopted portfolio architecture; neither draft layouts nor public coexistence resolves production governance.
- Select the official SKU and variant reference. Obtain documented dimensions, mechanism requirements or component compatibility only where the planned scene needs them.
- Product-specific claim substantiation and approved wording remain necessary; strategy decks, web copy and attractive technical illustrations are not substitutes.
- Across the full manifest, occupied massager and adjustable-bed sequences remain comparatively thin, despite the domestic device precedent in V004. Dynamic movement and continuity were not reviewed.
- Reference provenance, reuse permissions, casting decisions and actual production layouts remain unresolved. Website visuals and all category batches are supplied, but complete responsive journeys, videos and measured retail implementation are not.
