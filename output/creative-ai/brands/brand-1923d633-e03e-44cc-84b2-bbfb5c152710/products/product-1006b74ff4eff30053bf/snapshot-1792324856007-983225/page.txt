Prana is a premium medium soft mattress combining 100% natural latex with 3-zone pocket springs for ultimate comfort. What problem does Prana Pocket Spring Latex Mattress solve? It addresses multiple sleep concerns: partner disturbance, heat retention, allergen sensitivity (100% natural latex is hypoallergenic), and the need for responsive bounce combined with body support. 3. Who is Prana Pocket Spring Latex Mattress best for? Perfect for couples seeking zero motion transfer, buyers prioritizing sustainable materials, hot sleepers needing natural cooling, individuals with allergies, and anyone wanting premium comfort combining spring bounce with 100% natural latex support. 4. How does Prana Pocket Spring Latex Mattress work? The mattress features a 100% natural pin core latex layer (with small holes for air circulation) paired with 3-zone individually wrapped pocket springs. Each spring responds independently to your body's pressure, providing personalized support while preventing motion transfer; when one partner moves, the springs contain that movement locally. The organic cotton-infused fabric keeps the surface cool and fresh. 5. How is Prana Pocket Spring Latex Mattress different from alternatives?
