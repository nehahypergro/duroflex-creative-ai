    Product Description

    Introducing the ultimate savior for the bed of the clumsy! Say goodbye to soggy sheets and hello to a dry and cozy night's sleep with our waterproof mattress protector. With a luxurious bamboo feel, you'll feel like you're sleeping on a cloud while also reaping the benefits of our mattress protector's natural moisture-wicking properties. But that's not all! Our mattress protector also features elasticised corners, ensuring a snug fit around your mattress, no matter how much you toss and turn in your sleep. It's the perfect solution for those of you who value comfort and convenience. So go ahead, spill that glass of water or accidentally knock over your morning coffee. With our waterproof mattress protector, you can rest easy knowing that your mattress is protected from any mishaps. It is made from quality fabric that’s hypoallergenic and provides a healthier sleep environment by blocking fluids, moisture, dust mites, bed bugs, and stains from penetrating your mattress. It also helps circulate the air and transport moisture, directly impacting your comfort and sleep. It’s built to last and the moisture-wicking properties will keep it from overheating. The soft, breathable and sweat-absorbent mattress protector with no crinkly noise ensures comfort and reduces irritation to leave you feeling refreshed every morning. The mattress protector is machine washable. It is recommended to wash on a gentle machine cycle and tumble dry on low heat with linens and towels, or you can air-dry. TPU film with terry fabric allows air to pass through but blocks any fluids or liquids from seeping through to the mattress. The moisture-wicking properties of the fabric add to the micro-vented breathable mattress protector. The material is anti-allergenic, so it does not irritate the skin, and it’s durable.
