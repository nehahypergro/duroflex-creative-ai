Human-centred, young/vibrant, quirky, fabulous; Jester archetype centred on connection and enjoyment. Friendly wit, creative surprise and informal approachable language. Brand orange #F15A22, charcoal #414042, white (RGB supports #FFFFFF but printed hex conflicts). Migrena Grotesque for brand and Source Sans Pro for web, subject to actual font availability. Use actual architectural copy space and permitted logo treatment in scenes. Examples including serif social headlines and collage are examples, not a new universal typography or photo style rule.
