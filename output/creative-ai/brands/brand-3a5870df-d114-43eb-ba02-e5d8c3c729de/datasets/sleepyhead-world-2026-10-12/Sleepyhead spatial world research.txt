SPATIAL SYNTHESIS
The dominant language is optimistic, expressive and domestic. Soft rounded upholstery sits within recognisable rooms with windows, storage and activity zones. White/cream shells often allow pink, orange, yellow, cobalt, green and lilac to define furniture, wall planes or rugs; some rooms use a strong single-colour envelope. Pattern appears at a readable scale in checkerboards, stripes, waves and flowers. Timber, woven/ribbed/soft-textured surfaces temper glossy coloured lamps, transparent tables and ceramics. Daylight, plants, books and music/reading corners make spaces inhabited. A quieter subset proves that playful does not require saturation everywhere. Avoid reducing this collection to orange sofas, novelty props or a single maximalist formula.
